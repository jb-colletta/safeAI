# Honest claims

The most useful thing safeAI can teach is that safety controls differ in *kind*, not only in strength. That is why every answer is one of structural, procedural, or guided, and why the report ends with a short list of statements you can stand behind.

## The rule

**Never present a guided control as structural.** A funder, regulator, or technical reviewer will test it. A reviewer who discovers the difference on their own stops trusting everything else you told them.

## What to say

| Say | When |
|---|---|
| "Enforced in code" | Only for controls you answered *structural*, with evidence |
| "Requires approval from a named person" | For *procedural* controls |
| "We recommend" or "staff are asked to" | For *guided* controls |
| "The AI is a guest, not an admin" | When it is true: the AI can see and do only what the signed-in person can, and only what you have granted |
| "Here is what is in place, and here is what is coming" | With dates only once they are committed |
| "AI informs; people decide" | When a named person decides eligibility and priority |
| "The AI drafts; a person cares" | When care messages come from a named person |
| "We set floors for safeguarding and data; you write your values" | If you build software |

## What not to say

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| "100% safe", "provably ethical", "AI you never have to check" | Nobody can honestly promise this |
| "Enforced" for anything that depends on the AI following instructions | That is guided |
| Roadmap items in the present tense | They are not true yet |
| "Your data is never used for training" without naming the account type it depends on | Terms differ between consumer and business accounts |
| "The AI decides who needs help" or any eligibility language | See L4.07 |
| "AI companion", "counselor", or "care assistant" for members, clients, or constituents | See L6.03 |
| That your AI is "dignity-compliant", "ethical", or that it "upholds human dignity" | The floor shows safeguards you report having, not how the AI behaves. Say instead: "we assessed ourselves against a human-dignity floor and here is where we stand" |
| That you "meet the floor" while any floor control is unanswered or guided | The report calls that not met or not confirmed |
| That you are "aligned with", "approved by", or "compliant with" a standard or law, unless a qualified party has confirmed it | A framework or checklist is not a certification |

## A one-breath answer to "Is it safe?"

Fill this in from your report, and keep only the clauses your answers support:

> The AI is a guest, not an admin.
> **It sees** only what your people are already allowed to see, minus what you wall off.
> **It does** only what you grant, and it stops for a named person where it matters.
> **It says** nothing that contradicts what you stand for, and never poses as a person.
> **And you answer for it**, with a full record of every decision, and the ability to stop it.

The report marks each clause *supported*, *partly supported*, or *not yet supported* from your answers. For the clauses that are not yet supported, say so, and say what you are doing about it.

## Before you publish a claim

This is control L7.12: find the control behind the claim, check its guarantee type, check there is evidence, and say only what the evidence supports.

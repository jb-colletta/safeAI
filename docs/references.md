# References

safeAI is a practical checklist, not a restatement of any standard. These are well-known public frameworks and laws that cover parts of the same ground, listed so you can go deeper or map your answers to a framework your funder or regulator already uses.

**Verification note.** The entries below are given by name only, from the maintainers' general knowledge. We have not reproduced their text or checked current versions, links, or whether a newer edition exists. Before you cite one in a grant, contract, or policy, confirm the current title, version, and wording from the publisher. Laws vary by country, state, and sector, and some may not apply to you. This page is not legal advice.

## AI risk and management frameworks

| Source | Relevant to |
|---|---|
| NIST *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework* (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1) | Governance, mapping and measuring AI risk: levels 3, 4, 7 |
| ISO/IEC 42001, *AI management systems* | Accountability and governance: level 7 |
| OWASP *Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications* | Prompt injection, excessive agency, data leakage: levels 1 and 3 |

## Sector rules you must look up yourself

Safeguarding of children and vulnerable adults, health privacy, donor and financial records, and education records are governed by local law and by your funders, insurers, and professional or accrediting bodies. We deliberately do not list them: they differ too much by place, and the framework sets floors that sit *under* those rules, never in place of them.

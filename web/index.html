safeAI: Is it safe?

Safe AI is more than secure data. This free self-assessment walks through seven levels of the question a board, a funder, or the people you serve might ask, shows where the gaps are, and gives you a plan to close them.

Everything stays in this browser tab (and your browser's local storage). Nothing is uploaded.

About this assessment

safeAI is free and open source (Apache-2.0). This tool produces a planning aid from self-reported answers. It is not an audit, a certification, or legal advice.
